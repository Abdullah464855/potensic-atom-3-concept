# Potensic Atom 3 — Concept Landing Page

Day 4 of a 30-day drone landing page series: one drone, one landing page, every day. The first non-DJI drone of the series.

A concept redesign of a product landing page for the **Potensic Atom 3** camera drone — "The DJI alternative with a screen built into the controller." Built as a single self-contained static HTML file (all images inlined, no build step).

## What's on the page
- Light-grey page of large rounded cards (Eleviq-style layout): pill nav, left-aligned hero with a background-removed Atom 3 cutout over a rounded lakeside mask
- Stat row and 6-cell spec strip (under 249 g · 1/1.3-inch CMOS · 50 MP · 4K/60fps HDR · up to 40 min · 16 km transmission)
- The controller story in numbered rows: the RC PTD2's built-in 5.5-inch, 900-nit screen — no phone to clip in
- Camera section and a cinematic flight banner with the honesty notes (lab-rated flight times; the higher-capacity 50-minute battery can push takeoff weight over 249 g)
- The honesty bento: **no obstacle avoidance**, said plainly, with Beginner Mode and Return to Home as mitigations
- Pick your kit (UK guide prices): Standard **£339.99** / RC PTD2 screen controller **£499** / Fly More Combo (PTD2) **£579.99**
- Grouped full specifications and a 5-question FAQ, including why this trades sensing for sensor, screen and flight time
- Demo checkout pop-up

## Demo checkout
The checkout is a **design demo only** — there is no payment gateway, nothing is sent or stored, and no order is placed. It exists to demonstrate the page's conversion flow.

## Fonts
Open Sans (body/UI) and DM Sans ExtraBold (headlines) — the series pairing (the DJI Store pairing; DJI Sans is proprietary and DM Sans is the closest free match).
